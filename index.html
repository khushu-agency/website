FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about working with us.

Have specific technical or security questions? Our engineering leads are ready to discuss your specific infrastructure.

How do you protect our proprietary data and confidential IP?
We deploy models strictly within your Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) or on-premise infrastructure. No customer data is ever transmitted to public model endpoints or used for training without explicit cryptographic air-gapping. You retain 100% intellectual property ownership.
What is the typical timeline for an enterprise AI deployment?
A rapid proof-of-value (PoV) benchmark pilot is delivered in 2 to 3 weeks. Production enterprise integrations, including full security sign-off, typically range from 6 to 12 weeks depending on data complexity and API interfaces.
How do you ensure zero hallucinations in high-stakes environments?
We utilize deterministic guardrails, multi-agent cross-verification consensus protocols, citation anchoring, and continuous semantic drift monitoring with real-time confidence scores.
Can you integrate with our existing ERP, CRM, and cloud stack?
Yes. We build native connectors for AWS, GCP, Azure, Snowflake, Databricks, Salesforce, SAP, and custom REST/GraphQL services via secure OAuth and mTLS.

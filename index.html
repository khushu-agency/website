Do you only build AI systems?
No. Khushu combines digital experience design, websites, software, AI systems and automation. We choose the right combination based on the business problem rather than forcing every problem into an AI solution.
What kinds of businesses do you work with?
We work best with businesses that have a clear digital, operational or growth problem that can be improved through better experiences, software, AI or automation.
Can you improve our existing website or software?
Yes. We can redesign the experience, improve the interface, extend existing software or connect new AI and automation capabilities to systems you already use. Learn more →
Can you connect AI to our existing business tools?
Yes. Where APIs and permissions allow it, AI systems can be connected to CRMs, databases, support tools, communication platforms and other business systems. Learn more →
